Sandtraption
Privacy Policy

GrindWorks · Effective October 15, 2026

Sandtraption is a puzzle game made by GrindWorks ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

What we collect

We don't ask you to make an account, and we don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. We don't run our own servers: your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved on your device.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you use Google Play Games, the game uses it for cloud saves, achievements and leaderboards. Google signs you in with your Play Games profile and gives the game your Play Games player ID and display name. Your saved progress is stored in your Google account's Play Games saved data, and your achievements and leaderboard scores are shown to you and, depending on your Play Games settings, to other players. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn Play Games off or delete its data in the Play Games app or your Google account.

Device backup

Android may include the game's saved progress in your device's own backup to your Google account, so you can keep it on a new phone. You control this in your Android backup settings.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Ads never change how the game plays. Difficulty and scores are the same for every player, whether or not they watch ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase happened, but not your payment details.

Reminders

If you say yes, the game schedules reminders on your phone (for example when a free chest is ready). They are created on the device; no data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android's notification settings.

Children

Sandtraption is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or using Settings → Reset progress, deletes everything the game stored on your device. Cloud saves are removed through the Play Games app or your Google account. For data held by Google for advertising, see your Google account and Android ad settings. You can also email us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
