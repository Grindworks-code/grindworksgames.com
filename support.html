Frequently asked questions

How do I restore purchases?

Open Sandtraption while signed into the same Google Play account that made the purchase, then use the Restore purchases option in the shop. If the item does not return after a short wait, email us with your order number and we’ll investigate.

How do I report a bug?

Email us a short description of what happened, the steps that led to it, your device and Android version, and a screenshot or screen recording if you have one. The more we can reproduce, the faster we can fix it.
